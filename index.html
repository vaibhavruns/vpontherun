About me

Hi, I'm Vaibhav.

What started as a slow, ordinary jog in 2021 has, five years later, carried me from the streets of Mumbai to finish lines across India and the world.

Early on, I picked a number that scared me a little: 1,000 kilometres in a single year. I hit it in 2022, and somewhere in those miles the hobby quietly became a part of who I am.

I'm three marathons in now: Berlin, Mumbai, and Sydney. From here I'm chasing two things at once. The six Abbott World Majors I haven't run yet, and a faster version of myself along the way.
